Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Simple Junk Removal ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website simplejunkremoval.com and the associated chat widget. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you contact us or use our services.

Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, chat widget, or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address or general location
  • Details about the junk removal service you need

We collect this information only when you voluntarily provide it — for example, by filling out a contact form, using our chat widget, calling us, or texting us.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and provide a quote
  • Schedule and coordinate your junk removal service
  • Send follow-up messages related to your request or appointment
  • Communicate updates about your job or service

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not buy or sell leads.

SMS / Text Message Consent

By providing your phone number and initiating contact through our website chat widget, phone call, or text message, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Simple Junk Removal related to your inquiry, quote, or service appointment.

Message frequency varies based on your support needs. Standard message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further texts. For help, reply HELP or call us at 509-251-6096.

SMS opt-in consent is never shared with or sold to third parties.

Chat Widget

Our website uses a chat widget provided by GoHighLevel (leadconnectorhq.com). When you use the chat widget, information you provide may be stored and processed through GoHighLevel's platform to manage your inquiry. GoHighLevel's own privacy policy governs their handling of data.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how visitors use our site. This helps us improve the user experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not function as expected.

Data Retention

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill any legal obligations. If you would like your information removed from our records, please contact us using the information below.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
